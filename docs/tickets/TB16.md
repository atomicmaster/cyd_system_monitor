## Outcome

A touch or Host settings change applies once, survives restart, and resets affected radio evidence without inventing an Alert or observed recovery.

## Proposed ownership

Own configuration revision/NVS application, Settings UI, Host configuration CLI/IPC modules, and settings fixtures. Consume C02 revision/schema interfaces and the production scheduler/episode adapters.

## Scope

Implement display/sound settings, recalibration entry, independent radio controls, channel-plan presets, safe threshold values, and portable backup/import with validation and preview. Imported data excludes calibration, hardware/pairing identities, history, and artifacts.

## Resolve before promotion

Confirm portable schema and bounds, stale-write conflict behavior, idempotent retries, radio/rule change hooks, and ownership of per-rule threshold fragments so TB14/TB15 can work independently.

## Acceptance outline

- Concurrent touch/USB stale revisions are rejected and reloaded.
- Affected episodes end with configuration-changed reason; windows reset and any later episode needs fresh qualifying evidence.
- Disabled radios are labeled explicitly, preserve history, and reallocate schedule budget correctly.
- Channel-plan imports require no separate legal confirmation.
- Corrupt/unsupported imports do not partially change configuration.

## Verification to specify

Use revision/retry/import/NVS fixtures, Settings replay, and physical touch-versus-Host conflict/restart scenarios. Record exact fixture/scenario names, commands, and evidence paths when promoting this ticket.
