- One disabled radio gives its budget to the other; both disabled allow Host-only monitoring.
- No active scan/probe traffic is intentionally generated; initialization faults are surfaced for later Operational Alert integration.
- Dense traffic/UI/USB stress remains within accepted budgets or reopens the gate.
