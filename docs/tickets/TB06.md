# TB06 — Display battery charge and condition without blocking collection

- **Kind:** Tracer bullet
- **Detail:** Executable
- **Status:** Awaiting hardware — the Battery wire group, both codecs, macOS collector, Battery card, and simulator scenario are implemented and pass on the desktop (working tree, not yet committed). A physical display smoke and a battery-versus-batteryless Mac comparison are not recorded.
- **Milestones:** M3, M4
- **Depends on:** [C02](C02.md)
- **Board:** Exclusive final display smoke; Mac battery optional

Read [dispatch and completion rules](README.md#dispatch-and-completion).

## Outcome

The System detail shows battery charge and state, or explicit absence, while the slow condition source never blocks one-second Host updates.

## Design (as built)

- **Wire:** group key 7 "Battery" in [CORE_PROTOCOL.md](../../protocol/CORE_PROTOCOL.md#metric-groups-c02), ten required fields in two independent halves. Charge: availability, charge per-mille (0-1000), state (0 unknown, 1 charging, 2 discharging, 3 charged, 4 on AC not charging), minutes to empty/full (65535 unknown), charge source age (ms). Condition: availability, maximum capacity per-mille (0-1000; 0 = not reported), condition (0 unknown, 1 normal, 2 service recommended, 3 poor/replace), cycle count (65535 unknown), condition source age (ms). No freshness field. A batteryless host sends both availabilities Absent, never 0%. Largest group 38 bytes (limit 64); the maximum fixture frame is under 2,048.
- **Host pure pieces:** `host/metrics/src/battery.rs`: `charge_reading` (state and time mapping, impossible capacities are a broken read), and `parse_power_profile` with a small bounded JSON reader (no dependency added; `serde_json` is not in `Cargo.lock`). Input above 256 KiB, nesting above 24, more than 20,000 nodes, trailing data, and non-JSON are refused; wrongly typed, out-of-range, or missing fields make only that field unavailable. Fixtures in `host/metrics/fixtures/system_profiler/`.
- **Host adapter:** `host/platform-macos/src/battery.rs`. Charge: IOPowerSources (`IOPSCopyPowerSourcesInfo`, `InternalBattery`), refreshed every 30 s and at once when `IOPSGetProvidingPowerSourceType` changes (unplug/plug). Condition: `system_profiler SPPowerDataType -json` on a background thread, killed after 10 s, output capped at 256 KiB, refreshed hourly (retry after 5 min on failure); a run unreported after 15 s is abandoned and its late answer discarded. `collect` only reads caches and never waits. A failed refresh keeps the last good sample with its growing age.
- **Device:** `BatteryMetricState`. Charge is stale 90 s after its source sample, condition 3 h after (both from the reported age; a re-sent old sample stays stale). The source time is signed so a 3 h old sample received shortly after boot is not clamped to look fresh.
- **Card:** `Battery`, value `87% Charging` (states Charging, Draining, Charged, On AC; `Last ` prefix when retained), note `Health 91% Normal` (`Last 91% Normal` when stale), bar = charge. `--` plus `No battery`, `Read error`, or `Unavailable` otherwise; `Health Unavailable` when only the condition is missing. Absent is never dimmed. Time remaining and cycle count are on the wire but not shown (no room on the 154 px card).

## Acceptance

- Batteryless hosts show absent, never zero charge: fixture `battery-snapshot-v1-absent.hex`, `a_batteryless_host_is_absent_for_both_halves_never_zero`, `a_batteryless_host_is_absent_on_the_wire_never_zero_percent`, simulator step `batteryless host: absent`.
- A hung or malformed slow source cannot stall snapshots: `a_hung_slow_source_never_blocks_collect` (20 collections while the runner hangs take under 500 ms), `run_bounded_kills_a_hung_child_at_the_timeout`, `run_bounded_caps_output_and_reports_a_missing_program`, and the malformed/oversized/missing-section parser fixtures.
- Source age, unsupported fields, state changes, and errors survive transport and display: `a_battery_group_reaches_the_firmware_state_and_absence_is_not_zero` (C++/Rust link parity), `test_host_state.cpp` blocks 18-25 (charge 90 s, condition 3 h, re-sent old sample, early-boot old sample), and the simulator `battery-cards` transcript.
- C++ and Rust codecs agree on every `battery-*` row of `parity-matrix.txt` (0 mismatches).
- Every Battery value and note variant (states, conditions, per-mille extremes, fresh and `Last`) fits 148 px: `system-screen` measures them on a real card widget.
