# TB11 — Observe BLE advertisements through to an inspectable view

- **Kind:** Tracer bullet
- **Detail:** Outline
- **Status:** Not started
- **Milestones:** M5, M4
- **Depends on:** [TB09](TB09.md)
- **Board:** Exclusive radio/display smoke

Read [dispatch and completion rules](README.md#dispatch-and-completion). Refine this outline against prerequisite evidence before dispatch; it is not yet executable.

## Outcome

Passive BLE advertisements become density, Signal Strength, transmitter, and decoded-type views with honest identity and coverage labels.

## Proposed ownership

Own BLE advertisement parsing/aggregation modules, BLE fixtures, and `firmware/ui/ble/`. Consume TB09 events without editing the WiFi parser or scheduler.

## Scope

Implement bounded address/label retention, Signal Strength ordering, and documented beacon decoding. Supply advertisement-rate and identifier-change evidence for TB14/TB15; ordinary beacon discovery remains an observation.

## Resolve before promotion

Confirm supported advertising formats and duplicate handling, define eligible received-event counts, sanitize labels, and set bounded cardinality/loss behavior from F08 measurements.

## Acceptance outline

- Addresses are not physical-object identities and Signal Strength is not distance.
- Find My-pattern labels do not claim AirTag or tracker identity.
- Malformed or high-volume advertisements do not exhaust buffers or hide observation gaps.
- BLE and WiFi slices integrate without reducing Host/display behavior silently.

## Verification to specify

Use sanitized advertisement fixtures, BLE replay, and physical advertisement observation/load measurements. Record exact fixture/scenario names, commands, and evidence paths when promoting this ticket.
