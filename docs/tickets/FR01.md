# FR01 — Add and verify optional speaker alerts

- **Kind:** Feature request
- **Detail:** Outline
- **Status:** Deferred
- **Target:** V2 or later
- **Depends on:** Alert lifecycle and Settings interfaces from the MVP
- **Board:** Exclusive E32R28T session with a compatible speaker

## Outcome

The Monitor Device can optionally play a short, bounded sound for a new Alert, with the feature disabled by default and verified on the fitted E32R28T audio hardware.

## Background

M1 implemented a dormant GPIO4 amplifier-enable and DAC test-tone path, but no compatible speaker was available for acoustic verification. Speaker alerts are not important enough to hold the MVP hardware gate. F07 therefore records audio as an explicit deferred profile capability and links this feature request instead of treating it as passed.

The board schematic labels the amplifier `SC8002B`, while the vendor also supplies an `FM8002E` datasheet with different electrical limits. Implementation must use the actual fitted part and the analysis in [`audio-amplifier-comparison.md`](../../hardware/profiles/lcdwiki-esp32-32e-2.8/audio-amplifier-comparison.md); shared pinout or apparent firmware behavior is not evidence of electrical equivalence.

## Proposed scope

1. Identify the fitted amplifier and a compatible speaker/load.
2. Measure GPIO4 enable/disable behavior and DAC output on the physical board before relying on the existing test-tone path.
3. Add a persisted sound preference that defaults off.
4. Play a short sound for a new Alert without blocking display, touch, radio observation, USB handling, or Alert state transitions.
5. Stop sound emphasis on acknowledgment without ending or deleting the Alert.
6. Keep RGB, icon, text, and shape cues complete when sound is disabled or unavailable.

## Acceptance outline

- Enable polarity, output waveform, speaker/load compatibility, and acoustic output are measured on the fitted board.
- Sound remains off after first start and firmware update until the Operator explicitly enables it.
- Alert sound duration and rate are bounded; repeated Alerts cannot create an unbounded or continuous tone.
- Acknowledgment silences the sound while preserving the Alert lifecycle and evidence.
- Disabling sound, an absent speaker, or an audio fault does not weaken visual Alert meaning or block monitoring.
- Combined-load testing shows no material regression in touch latency, display behavior, radio coverage, USB traffic, or memory headroom.

## Verification to specify

Promote this request only when a compatible speaker and basic electrical measurement equipment are available. Record the speaker/load, fitted amplifier marking, GPIO4 levels, DAC waveform or frequency, sound preference persistence, Alert lifecycle scenarios, and combined-load results.
