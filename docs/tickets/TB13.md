## Outcome

Committed Alert/Baseline evidence survives reset; previously active episodes return as interrupted, and storage damage or pressure remains visible and bounded.

## Proposed ownership

Own the flash journal adapter, schema migration/reclamation, persistent boot/record ordering, bounded Baseline retention, aggregate Capacity-pressure support, and persistence fault integration. Consume TB12 episode and C02 storage interfaces.

## Scope

Implement checksummed append/commit/recovery, enforced capacities derived from F08, reserved Operational/aggregate storage, corruption reporting, LRU baselines, and maximum-write behavior under hostile identifier volume. Connect concrete protocol/radio/persistence/repeated-reset faults to Operational Alerts without Confidence.

## Resolve before promotion

Finalize maximum record sizes, commit/checkpoint semantics, reclamation protocol, wear assumptions, migration fixtures, and reboot ordering before implementation. Split this outline into dependent children if journal correctness and capacity behavior cannot remain reviewable together; preserve the full durable-evidence outcome as the parent gate.

## Acceptance outline

- Power cuts during append and reclamation preserve committed evidence; torn/corrupt entries report loss.
- Prior active episodes become interrupted without invented quiet, recovery time, or exact outage time; fresh evidence creates new episodes.
- Detailed capacity exhaustion coalesces by rule and cannot crowd out Operational reporting.
- Rapid identity churn and repeated restarts remain within confirmed memory/flash/write budgets.
- Operational faults are distinct from ordinary disconnect, denied permission, or Operator-disabled status.

## Verification to specify

Use storage-adapter fault injection and migration/capacity replay, then physical interruption tests and measured write-rate evidence. Record exact fixture/scenario names, commands, and evidence paths when promoting this ticket.
