## Outcome

The Storage card shows root filesystem capacity and physical-device read/write rates, with correct unavailable states at startup and wake.

## Proposed ownership

Own macOS root-storage collection, storage-specific scenarios, and `firmware/ui/storage/`. Shared snapshot/schema edits remain with the contract owner.

## Scope

Follow [Host metrics](../host-metrics.md) for APFS capacity and backing-device counters. Carry slow capacity timestamps separately from one-second I/O rates through snapshots to current detail.

## Resolve before promotion

Validate root-to-physical-driver mapping on the supported Mac and fixtures for missing statistics, counter resets, multiple volumes, and sleep/wake. Establish per-field bounds and maximum card text lengths.

## Acceptance outline

- APFS volumes are not summed as separate disks.
- First samples and counter resets are temporarily unavailable rather than zero rates.
- Hotplug/wake/remapping clears the baseline, and stale capacity does not become fresh merely because a new snapshot arrives.
