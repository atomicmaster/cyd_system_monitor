# TB04 — Display root storage capacity and disk activity

- **Kind:** Tracer bullet
- **Detail:** Executable
- **Status:** Awaiting hardware — Storage wire group, both codecs, macOS collector, Storage card, and simulator scenario are implemented and pass on the desktop (working tree, not yet committed). A physical display smoke and a real storage-activity demonstration are not recorded.
- **Milestones:** M3, M4
- **Depends on:** [C02](C02.md)
- **Board:** Exclusive final display smoke

Read [dispatch and completion rules](README.md#dispatch-and-completion).

## Outcome

The Storage card shows root filesystem capacity and physical-device read/write rates, with correct unavailable states at startup and wake.

## Design (as built)

- **Wire:** group key 6 "Storage" in [CORE_PROTOCOL.md](../../protocol/CORE_PROTOCOL.md#metric-groups-c02): capacity availability, total bytes, available bytes, capacity source age (ms), I/O availability, read bytes/s, write bytes/s, I/O source age (ms). All eight required; no freshness field. Capacity and I/O availability are independent; a half that is not Available carries zero values. Largest group 58 bytes (limit 64); the maximum-size fixture frame is 106 bytes against the 2,048 limit.
- **Host:** `host/metrics` has `StorageCapacityReading`, the pure `DiskIoSampler` (unavailable on first sample, counter decrease, non-monotonic clock, gap over `CPU_MAX_SAMPLE_GAP_MS`, or zero elapsed; re-baselines every time), and the pure `whole_disk_name` / `physical_disk_name` mapping. `host/platform-macos/src/storage.rs` reads `statfs("/")` (30 s cache with its own age) and the IOKit `IOBlockStorageDriver` `Statistics` ("Bytes (Read)", "Bytes (Write)") by walking the I/O Registry from the `IOMedia` of `/`'s device up to the physical disk's driver (`disk3s1s1` on container `disk3` on physical `disk0`). Public APIs only, no new dependency. The driver is re-resolved every 30 reads; a changed device re-baselines the sampler.
- **Device:** `StorageMetricState` on `HostState`. I/O is stale 3 s after it was received (age-adjusted); capacity is stale 90 s after its source sample (received time minus reported age), so a snapshot that re-carries an old capacity sample does not become fresh.
- **Card:** `Storage`, value `used / total GB` (decimal, as Finder; TB from 1 TB; at most 21 characters below 1 PB, 31 at the wire bound), note `R 12.3 MB/s W 4.5 MB/s` (at most 30 characters with `Last `), `I/O Unavailable` when only I/O is missing, `--` plus wording when capacity is missing. Stale retains, dims, and prefixes `Last `.

## Owned modules

`protocol/` group row, fixtures and both codecs; `host/metrics`, `host/platform-macos/src/storage.rs`, the `link.rs` collector line; `firmware/domain/src/cards/storage_card.cpp`, `StorageMetricState`; `simulator/scenarios/storage-cards.expected` and `storage_cards_scenario.cpp`.

## Acceptance

- APFS volumes are not summed: every volume of a container resolves to one physical disk (`apfs_volumes_resolve_to_one_physical_disk_and_are_not_summed`); capacity is the shared container space.
- First samples, counter resets, wake gaps, and remapped devices are temporarily unavailable, never a zero rate; an idle disk is a measured zero.
- Stale capacity does not become fresh because a new snapshot arrives (`test_host_state.cpp` block 11-17, simulator `capacity sample is 91 s old`).
- C++ and Rust codecs agree on every `storage-*` row of `parity-matrix.txt` (0 mismatches).
