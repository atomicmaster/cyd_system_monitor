# Retain radio addresses locally

The Monitor Device may retain raw WiFi and Bluetooth addresses that were visible over the air rather than replace them with keyed pseudonyms. Raw addresses make recurrence, vendor-prefix interpretation, and operator inspection straightforward on a personal local appliance. Radio History remains authoritative on the Monitor Device and is never synchronized automatically; an explicit CLI export may retrieve raw addresses together with Alerts, coverage metadata, and schema/firmware versions after an on-screen disclosure.
