# Schedule and disclose radio coverage

2.4 GHz WiFi and BLE observation will run in explicit Observation Windows rather than claim continuous simultaneous monitoring. The Monitor Device will expose its current observation mode and relevant Coverage Gaps because its single RF module time-shares WiFi and Bluetooth and cannot provide complete continuous coverage. The early M1a combined hardware gate must establish switching overhead and coverage measurement limits; requested window duration is not automatically verified receive time. This makes the product's evidence and absences interpretable instead of hiding missed airtime behind best-effort concurrency. The E32R28T cannot observe 5/6 GHz WiFi, so Host-targeted radio correlation requires relevant captured evidence and unsupported or unknown Host coverage stays visible.
